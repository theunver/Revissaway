# WhatsApp Business Automated Messages Setup Guide
## RevissaWay

This guide will help you set up automated messages for your WhatsApp Business account using WhatsApp Business Manager.

---

## 📋 Prerequisites

1. **WhatsApp Business Account** activated
2. **WhatsApp Business Manager** access
3. **Meta Business Suite** account
4. Phone number verified: **+61 435 955 386**

---

## 🔧 Setup Instructions

### **Step 1: Access WhatsApp Business Manager**

1. Go to https://business.facebook.com/
2. Select your RevissaWay Business Account
3. Navigate to **WhatsApp Business Manager**
4. Click on **"Message Templates"** or **"Automation"**

---

### **Step 2: Configure Business Hours**

1. Go to **Settings** → **Business Hours**
2. Set the following schedule:

```
Monday - Friday: 09:00 - 19:00 (AEST/AEDT - Melbourne time)
Saturday: 10:00 - 16:00 (AEST/AEDT)
Sunday: Closed
```

3. Enable **"Away Message"** for outside business hours

---

### **Step 3: Create Welcome Message**

**Location:** Settings → Messaging → Greeting Message

**Message Content:**
```
Hello 👋 and welcome to RevissaWay — Australia's trusted bridge to world-class medical care in Turkey 🇹🇷

Thank you for reaching out!

Our team will respond shortly.

If your inquiry is about:
• Hair Transplant 💇‍♂️ — please type 1
• Dental Treatment 😁 — please type 2
• Cosmetic Surgery 💉 — please type 3
• Travel & Hotel Packages ✈️ — please type 4

You can also email us anytime at info@revissaway.com.au.
```

**Settings:**
- ✅ Enable: "Send greeting message when contact first messages you"
- ✅ Enable: "Send greeting message outside business hours"

---

### **Step 4: Create Away Message (Auto Reply)**

**Location:** Settings → Messaging → Away Message

**Message Content:**
```
Hi again 👋 this is an automated message from RevissaWay.

Our representatives are currently assisting other clients or outside working hours, but don't worry — your message has been received ✅

We'll get back to you as soon as possible (usually within 1–3 hours).

In the meantime, you can visit 🌐 www.revissaway.com.au for general information and free consultation.

For urgent matters, please email info@revissaway.com.au
```

**Settings:**
- ✅ Enable: "Send away message only outside business hours"
- ⏰ Set delay: 30 minutes (if available, otherwise use default)

---

### **Step 5: Add Language Support**

**Add to Welcome Message:**
```
(Primary language: English)

For Turkish / Türkçe: Please type "5" 🇹🇷 / Türkçe iletişim için 5 yazabilirsiniz.
```

---

### **Step 7: Configure Chat Automation (Advanced)**

If available in your WhatsApp Business plan:

1. **Go to:** Automation → Flow Builder
2. **Create flow:** "Service Inquiry"
3. **Triggers:**
   - Customer types "1" → Assign to Hair Transplant team
   - Customer types "2" → Assign to Dental team
   - Customer types "3" → Assign to Cosmetic Surgery team
   - Customer types "4" → Assign to Travel coordinator
   - Customer types "5" → Assign to Turkish-speaking agent

## 🎯 Best Practices

1. **Respond within 1-3 hours** during business hours
2. **Monitor chat history** for common questions
3. **Update quick replies** based on frequently asked questions
4. **Set up notifications** for new messages
5. **Train team** on professional medical communication
6. **Use emojis sparingly** but effectively for friendly tone
7. **Always include contact alternatives** (email, website)
